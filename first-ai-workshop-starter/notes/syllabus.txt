CS201 Data Structures - Course Syllabus (Autumn)

Assessment. The course is assessed by four programming assignments (40%), one
mid-semester test (20%) and a final examination (40%). There is no separate
attendance component, but see the attendance rule below.

Late submission. Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 10% of the
available marks per calendar day, up to a maximum of three days. After three
days the submission is not accepted and is recorded as zero. Extensions must be
requested in writing at least 48 hours before the deadline, and are granted only
for documented medical or family emergencies. Laptop failure is not grounds for
an extension; use version control.

Resubmission. A student who scores below 40% on an assignment may resubmit it
once, within seven days of receiving feedback. The resubmitted mark is capped at
40%. Resubmission is not available for the mid-semester test.

Attendance. Students must attend at least 75% of scheduled lab sessions to be
eligible to sit the final examination. Labs missed for documented medical
reasons do not count against this total.

Academic integrity. You may discuss approaches with classmates, but all
submitted code must be your own. You must declare any use of AI coding tools in
a comment at the top of each submitted file, stating which tool and what it was
used for. Undeclared use is treated as plagiarism.

Office hours. Tuesdays 14:00-16:00 in Room B214, or by appointment.
